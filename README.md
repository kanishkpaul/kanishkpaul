## whoami

I build at the edge of **AGI/ASI**, with a deep obsession for **reasoning**, **intelligence**, **agency**, and the philosophy that starts to matter when models stop looking like autocomplete and start looking like minds.

I care about first-principles thinking, ambitious systems, and turning raw model capability into real leverage. My lane is simple: build machines that **reason better, act better, and think with more structure** — usually by connecting fields that don't normally talk to each other (**category theory, sheaves, causal systems, world models**) to problems in machine intelligence.

When it helps, I challenge assumptions a level above first principles. If that sounds intense — good. That's the point.

## what i'm locked in on

- **neurosymbolic reasoning** — chain-of-thought-style reasoning with real structure underneath
- **categorical & sheaf-theoretic AI** — using compositional math as the backbone of intelligence, not decoration
- **world models & agency** — online rule induction, uncertainty, planning, and computer-use agents
- **agi/asi & alignment-adjacent questions** — philosophy of mind, consciousness, and what "understanding" actually costs
- shipping tools that feel sharp, useful, and a little unfair

## 🔬 research

Independent research, reported honestly — negative results included. Method and datasets on the flagship work are held back pending publication; each repo shows the design, real numbers, and runnable infrastructure. Write-ups at [kanishkpaul.com/research](https://kanishkpaul.com/research).

| project | the idea | what's shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [register-obstruction](https://github.com/kanishkpaul/register-obstruction) | Measuring honorific-register loss across direct and English-pivot Bengali→Hindi translation, with a preregistered test of a contextual-obstruction predictor. The predictor did not survive robustness analysis; the measurement result remains. | aggregate evidence + classifier demo + high-level negative verdict; `paper in prep` |
| [arc-agi3-world-models](https://github.com/kanishkpaul/arc-agi3-world-models) | **World-model agents for ARC-AGI-3** — can an agent induce a game's hidden rules and plan in its own head? Online rule induction, uncertainty probing, planning. | runnable eval substrate, `WorldModel` protocol, 25 passing tests |
| [butterflygate](https://github.com/kanishkpaul/butterflygate) | A **sub-quadratic O(n log n)** replacement for self-attention. | forward-pass benchmark + crossover figure; mechanism withheld |

> More lives in the lab and isn't public yet — **category-theoretic AI targeting ARC-AGI-3**, **internal world models**, and **context-aware causal cognitive control**. Private until there's a paper to point to.
